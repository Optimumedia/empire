How to play

  1. Four walls: 08:30 drop-off → train, 10:00 work, 20:00 hard stop, 23:00 lights out.
  2. Tap missions as you do them. Earn 72% of the board and the day is won. Rest days are fine.
  3. Missions mint Grit. Grit builds your City and buys real rewards. The City earns Bucks on its own. You start with 120 Grit — enough for an HQ and a Home.
  4. Lost a day? A shield covers it. Missed twice? Tomorrow's bar drops to 50%. Sick kid? Skip the day.
  5. Every Sunday the Council reviews your week and proposes changes. You accept or decline.

How the City works

Four districts mirror your four stats. Buildings cost Grit — you can only earn Grit by doing real missions. Each district's vitality is your last 7 days in that stat: neglect Body and Riverside goes dormant and earns half. Neighbours from different districts pay +10% each — a balanced life is literally more profitable. Bucks buy decor and boosts. Three Ad Auctions a day, no more.

The rules

Missions pay XP into four stats and mint Grit 1:1 (+100 Grit per day won). Win the day at the Council's threshold — 72% by default, 50% the day after a loss. Shields cover lost days; skipped days don't count. Beat the ghost and next week pays +10% XP (stacks to ×1.3). Five days won unlocks the weekly crate. Iron Week can be staked: 300 Grit, back ×1.5 on success, burned on failure. Levels never go down.
