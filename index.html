All-time

How the City works

Draw roads from City Hall, zone land for homes, shops and industry, and place services with a reach. Zoned tiles fill in on their own — 2 hours to the first house, a day to mid-rise, three days to towers — while they have a road within 2 tiles, power, demand, and the services each level asks for. Tap a tile to see what it's waiting on; a crane means it's building. Land & tax buys more land (first ring at 50 people) and sets the tax rate; the corner buttons zoom. Everything structural costs Grit, which only missions mint. Taxes pay Bucks for trees, lights, plazas and festivals. Your stats are the weather: Family speeds homes and lifts happiness, Business drives shops and taxes, Body stretches parks, Health thins pollution. A lazy week stalls the city; it never wrecks it.

Your own mission

The rules

Missions pay XP into four stats and mint Grit 1:1 (+100 Grit per day won). Win the day at the Council's threshold — 72% by default, 50% the day after a loss. Shields cover lost days; skipped days don't count. Beat the ghost and next week pays +10% XP (stacks to ×1.3). Five days won unlocks the weekly crate. Iron Week can be staked: 300 Grit, back ×1.5 on success. Levels never go down, and each one from 2 to 10 unlocks a perk — see the ladder under All-time.
